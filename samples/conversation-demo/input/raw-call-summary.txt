Summary of the follow-up call held on Tuesday afternoon.

The call opened with Asha Rao confirming that she had received the discharge papers from Lakeview Memorial Hospital. She explained that her husband, Vikram Rao, had been admitted last week after a fall at home and that the ward doctors had since diagnosed him with type 2 diabetes and early stage chronic kidney disease. Asha said the family had found the first few days difficult because Vikram was not used to managing his own medication.

Asha asked whether the home-care nurse could visit them at 14 Hillcrest Lane, Koramangala, Bengaluru 560034 rather than at her sister's flat, since Vikram would be staying at home for the next month. She also mentioned that their daughter, Priya, would be the main contact on weekdays and could be reached on 98450 12345 or at priya.rao@example.test.

Later in the call Mrs. Rao raised a billing concern. The hospital had charged INR 48,250.00 for the admission, but the insurer, Bluepeak Health Insurance, had so far only approved part of it. She read out the policy member number MRN-778213 and asked us to chase the claim.

Before closing, Dr. Kavya Menon from the care coordination team joined briefly and agreed to review Vikram's insulin plan. Asha thanked everyone and asked that all future letters be sent to the Hillcrest Lane address. Next steps: Kavya to call Priya on Friday; the billing team to follow up with Bluepeak; Asha to share the latest blood test results by email.
